a letter you weren't expecting ♡

Hey, you. There's something I want you to know.

I know there might be a lot going on in your mind right now. Things you haven't told anyone, questions you don't have answers to, and worries that seem to follow you even when everything around you gets quiet.

Maybe sometimes it's easier to say you're okay than to explain what's really happening. Maybe you don't even know where to begin, because there's just so much to say.

But you don't have to find all the words tonight. You don't have to turn every feeling into an explanation, and you don't have to solve every problem before allowing yourself to rest.

I hope you stop being so hard on yourself for not having everything figured out. You're still learning, still growing, and still finding your way through a world that can feel overwhelming sometimes.

Your future matters, yes. Your dreams matter, too. But so does the person you are while you're trying to reach them. You deserve kindness even on the days when you feel unproductive, uncertain, or far behind where you hoped to be.

And if the things you're carrying ever become too difficult to manage alone, please remember that you deserve support. You don't need a perfect explanation to tell someone you trust that you've been having a hard time.

For now, imagine sitting by the sea at night. The air is cool, the waves keep moving, and for a little while, nothing is asking you to have all the answers.

Let tomorrow wait a little. You can come back to your worries when you've had some rest. One step is still a step, even when it's a small one.

I hope that one day, when you look back at this chapter of your life, you realize that you made it through things you once didn't know how to put into words.

Until then, please remember to leave a little kindness for yourself, too.

Take care, okay? — Claire ♡
